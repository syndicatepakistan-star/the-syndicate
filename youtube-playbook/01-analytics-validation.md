# Analytics Validation (Post-Launch)

Replace industry CPM/RPM averages with **your** numbers. Do this after monetization (or once you have enough views to see geography). Re-run monthly.

## Where to look in YouTube Studio

### A. Audience geography

1. Open [YouTube Studio](https://studio.youtube.com) → **Analytics** → **Audience**.
2. Find **Top geographies** (watch time % and views % by country).
3. Record your top 10 countries in the tracker below.
4. Goal for this channel: rising share from **US, UK, AU, CA** (Tier-1). UAE is a strong secondary.

### B. Revenue by geography (after YPP / AdSense)

1. **Analytics** → **Revenue**.
2. Use the **Geography** filter (or country breakdown if shown).
3. Note **estimated revenue** and **RPM** per country when available.
4. Compare: high views from Pakistan/India + low revenue = geography ceiling, not “bad videos.”

### C. When viewers are on YouTube (heatmap)

1. **Analytics** → **Audience** → **When your viewers are on YouTube**.
2. Darkest cells = peak watch windows in **your viewers’** local patterns (Studio shows them relative to your account settings — cross-check with upload times in [03-upload-schedule.md](./03-upload-schedule.md)).
3. After each month, adjust schedule only if the heatmap consistently disagrees with the locked Tue–Thu ET window.

### D. Per-video geography (double down)

1. Open a video → **Analytics** → Audience / Advanced mode.
2. Note which titles pull more US/UK watch time.
3. Make more videos like those; deprioritize formats that only pull Tier-4 traffic.

## How to interpret (quick rules)

| Signal | Meaning | Action |
|--------|---------|--------|
| Tier-1 watch time rising | Content + SEO working | Keep English SaaS/AI angle; clone winning titles |
| Views up, RPM flat/down | More low-CPM countries | Tighten keywords to US/UK problems; USD/GBP examples |
| UAE strong, US weak | Business English OK, not US-search yet | Add “for startups / USD / Stripe / AWS” style queries |
| Heatmap peaks ≠ upload time | Wrong first-hour audience | Move schedule 1–2h before new peak (see upload schedule) |
