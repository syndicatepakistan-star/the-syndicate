**How to use:** Pick one title → validate with YouTube autocomplete → film 10–15+ min long-form → clip 1–2 Shorts. Prefer USD/GBP examples in the video even if the title is country-neutral.

Content mix reminder: ~70% SaaS/AI tutorials & comparisons, ~20% build-in-public / career-tech, ~10% fintech-app explainers.

## Production checklist (per title)

- [ ] Confirm autocomplete / similar videos exist (demand)
- [ ] Title ≤ ~60–70 characters where possible; keyword first
- [ ] Thumbnail: face + product UI + 3–4 words max
- [ ] Hook in first 15 seconds; chapters; CTA to next video
- [ ] Mention USD or GBP pricing when comparing paid tools
- [ ] Length 10–15+ minutes for mid-rolls
- [ ] After publish: check geography in Studio (see [01-analytics-validation.md](./01-analytics-validation.md))

## Country flavor (optional title variants)

Swap or append when targeting a specific market:

- USA: add `USA`, `for startups`, `USD`, Stripe/Shopify/AWS
- UK: add `UK`, `GBP`, `HMRC`, `best UK alternative`
- UAE: add `for freelancers in Dubai`, `multi-currency`, keep English
