# Locked Upload Schedule (Tue–Thu · US Eastern Afternoon)

**Decision locked:** Publish long-form on **Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday**, timed for **US Eastern afternoon** (primary Tier-1 overlap). Review the Studio heatmap monthly; only change the window if data consistently disagrees.

## Locked window

| Setting | Value |
|---------|--------|
| Days | **Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday** (pick 1 day/week and stay consistent) |
| Audience timezone for scheduling | **America/New_York (ET)** |
| Publish time (ET) | **3:00 PM ET** |
| Upload / finish processing by | **1:00–2:00 PM ET** (1–2 hours before peak) |
| Content type | Long-form (10–15+ min); Shorts separately in evenings local-to-audience |

### Pakistan time (PKT = UTC+5)

| Season | 3:00 PM ET equals | Finish upload/process by (PKT) | Go-live (PKT) |
|--------|-------------------|-------------------------------|---------------|
| **ET (winter, UTC−5)** ~ early Nov – mid Mar | **1:00 AM PKT** (next calendar day) | **11:00 PM–12:00 AM PKT** | **1:00 AM PKT** |
| **EDT (summer, UTC−4)** ~ mid Mar – early Nov | **12:00 AM midnight PKT** (next calendar day) | **10:00–11:00 PM PKT** | **12:00 AM PKT** |

**Practical habit:** In YouTube Studio → Upload → **Schedule**, set timezone to **Eastern Time**, schedule **Tue/Wed/Thu 3:00 PM**. Ignore PKT math after that.

This window also roughly covers **UK late afternoon / early evening** (winter: ~8 PM GMT; summer: ~8 PM BST), which is acceptable Tier-1 overlap. UAE evening is secondary — do not split the schedule unless Analytics shows UAE as your #1 market.

## Weekly cadence (recommended)

| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| Mon | Edit thumbnail/title; queue Schedule for Tue/Wed/Thu 3 PM ET |
| Tue–Thu | Video goes live at 3 PM ET; reply to early comments in first 2 hours |
| Fri | Cut 3–5 Shorts from long-form; post Shorts evening (audience local) |
| Weekend | Optional: research next title from [02-niche-keyword-titles.md](./02-niche-keyword-titles.md) |

**Default:** 1 long-form per week on **Wednesday 3:00 PM ET**.

## Monthly heatmap review (required)

On the **1st of each month** (or after every 4 uploads):

1. Studio → **Analytics** → **Audience** → **When your viewers are on YouTube**.
2. Note the darkest weekday blocks.
3. Fill the log:

```
Month: YYYY-MM
Locked schedule still: Wed 3:00 PM ET? YES / NO
Heatmap peak weekdays: _______________
Heatmap peak hours (as shown): _______________
Tier-1 watch time % (US+UK+AU+CA): ____%
Decision: KEEP schedule / SHIFT ±1–2 hours to: ________
Reason: ________________________________
```

4. Rules:
   - **KEEP** if peaks are within ~2 hours of 3 PM ET or engagement is healthy.
   - **SHIFT** only after **2 consecutive months** of clear mismatch (e.g. peaks at 10 AM ET only).
   - Do not chase UAE or Pakistan peaks if Tier-1 % is the goal.

Cross-check geography/RPM with [01-analytics-validation.md](./01-analytics-validation.md).
